# Block 4 prior art: forecast-driven PER-safe action selection with an ACK-calibrated margin

Block 4 under test: per packet, choose a = (M ∈ {QPSK, 16-QAM}, N_sym, pilot set 𝒫). p̂_fail(a) = Pr over the *forecast distribution of the Doppler-drift error e over the receive interval* that the residual leaves a calibrated tolerance (SNR_eff = SNR/(1+SNR·φ_a(e))). Safe set: p̂_fail ≤ α − m_t, where m_t is updated from delayed ACKs (OLLA / ACI). Pick the action with the highest B_a(1−p̂)/T_a; if none is safe, fall back, wait or drop. Not learned online.

## Q1. UWA AMC that uses Doppler, velocity, motion or a Doppler forecast as input, predicts ahead to counter feedback delay, or uses a PER target with a margin or outer loop

### Takeaway
No UWA work found combines the three defining parts of Block 4: (i) a forecast distribution of the *Doppler drift* as the predictor of PER, (ii) packet length and pilot layout as actions next to modulation, and (iii) a PER-target safe set whose margin is updated online from delayed ACKs. Each part has precedents on its own:
- **Doppler as an AMC input** exists only as a measured feature, table index or physical constraint: Park 2026, Anjangi–Chitre 2018, Mangione 2021, Jeong 2025, Chude-Okonkwo 2013.
- **Predicting one travel time or one round trip ahead** to beat feedback delay is well established. Radosevic et al. even model Doppler with velocity and acceleration terms, but inside the receiver, to compensate motion before predicting the path gains; the forecast does not feed the decision.
- **Uncertainty-aware PER/BER safety** exists: Park 2026 has a PER target with a prediction-uncertainty margin (tuned offline, fixed); Wu–Chitre–Anjangi 2025 select MCS on an upper quantile of the BER (Quantile Absolute Deviation, QAD) that is close to conformal. Neither updates its margin from ACK outcomes.

The closest single paper on the *decision structure* is Park et al. 2026 (MDPI, so excluded from the library). The closest on *physics plus uncertainty* is Wu–Chitre–Anjangi, JOE 2025.

### Inferences
- Novelty that still stands for Block 4: (a) PER predicted from a *Doppler-drift forecast distribution integrated over the receive interval*; (b) N_sym and pilot positions as actions under that forecast; (c) a safe set whose margin is updated from delayed ACKs. No UWA paper found does any one of (a) to (c), let alone all three.
- Park 2026 is the paper a reviewer is most likely to raise against "PER-target + prediction + uncertainty margin + Doppler input". Distinguish Block 4 on three points: the margin is learned online from ACKs, not swept offline; the actions are frame layout and modulation, not fountain-code overhead; and the predictor is a kinematic forecast of the drift, not a CSI predictor with Doppler as a LUT index. Note it is MDPI, excluded from the library by the owner's rule; cite it anyway as prior art.
- The QAD bound in Wu–Chitre–Anjangi 2025 is effectively a split-conformal upper bound on BER. Block 4's "calibrated tolerance" should be positioned against it explicitly: a static residual quantile versus an online, ACK-driven, ACI-style margin with a long-run PER guarantee.
- Radosevic et al. already model Doppler with velocity and acceleration over several packets. That weakens any claim that "kinematic Doppler modeling" itself is new; the new part is fusing both ends and using the forecast for the frame and the decision.

## Q2. Adaptive packet or frame length for UWA under time variation or Doppler

### Takeaway
I found no UWA work that adapts packet length (number of OFDM symbols) or pilot layout per packet from a Doppler or motion forecast. UWA packet-size work optimizes for BER/FEC, distance and ARQ efficiency, not for Doppler drift. The motion-related frame adaptations found change OFDM numerology (subcarrier spacing, CP, number of subcarriers), not length, and use instantaneous estimates. The nearest analogue is terrestrial: MoFA (Wi-Fi A-MPDU length adapted to mobility).

### Cited Findings
- Chude-Okonkwo et al. 2013 adapt "the subcarrier spacing and guard interval" to receiver movement (numerology, not length). — [UJ repository](https://pure.uj.ac.za/en/publications/adaptive-transmission-technique-for-short-range-mobile-underwater/)
- Mangione et al. 2021 calibrate "the prefix length and the number of sub-carriers" against the Doppler spread. — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/access.2021.3082766)
- Kim & Lee, Applied Acoustics 2025, DOI 10.1016/j.apacoust.2025.110554. OCFDM "reduces packet length by replacing an outer preamble… with an inner preamble" for short-TTI UUV links. This shortens packets by design, not adaptively. — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apacoust.2025.110554)
- UWA packet-size optimization concerns BER, FEC, distance and ARQ: "Optimal packet size depends on the bit error rate of the channel and the FEC code used"; trade-off "between the channel efficiency and the packet error rate, both increasing with increasing packet size". Stojanovic's ARQ data-link optimization controls packet size for ARQ efficiency. — [Akyildiz group (packet size, INFOCOM)](https://ianakyildiz.com/bwn/sensor16/reading/packetSize_Infocom_camera_ready.pdf); [Stojanovic, "Optimization of a Data Link Protocol for an Underwater Acoustic Channel"](https://millitsa.coe.neu.edu/publications/conference/arq.pdf)
- In the local library, "packet length" + Doppler returns only three unrelated records (OCFDM, Bayesian resampling, temporal coherence); "packet size" adaptive returns routing and energy papers (e.g., APSSR 2021). — local DB search
- Terrestrial analogue, MoFA: "a standard-compliant mobility-aware A-MPDU… length adaptation scheme for Wi-Fi". "Mobility intensifies the time-varying nature of the wireless channel, making the channel estimation conducted only at the beginning of frame reception insufficient to ensure robust delivery of long A-MPDU frames"; "1.8x higher" throughput than a fixed 10 ms. Patent US10477433B2, "Mobility-aware frame aggregation". — [Google Patents US10477433](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10477433), [academia.edu MoFA](https://www.academia.edu/94935705/MoFA)

### Inferences
- MoFA is the best terrestrial precedent for "packet length chosen against within-packet channel drift caused by mobility". It detects mobility reactively from CSI and does not forecast. Block 4's N_sym choice, driven by a forecast drift distribution and paired with pilot placement, has no direct UWA precedent found.
- Shorter packets as a remedy for Doppler drift appear only as fixed design choices (sTTI OCFDM), not as a per-packet decision.

### Gaps
- Bibliographic details of the MoFA conference paper (by memory: Byeon, Yoon, Lee, Choi, Cho, Oh, ACM CoNEXT 2014) were not verified. An OpenAlex search did not return it; cite via the patent or verify before use.
- Basagni/Petrioli-style UWA "optimized packet size selection" (JOE) papers were not retrieved; they are believed to be BER/energy-based, not Doppler-based.

## Q3. Outer-loop margin from delayed ACKs in UWA; conformal or calibrated PER prediction in link adaptation (terrestrial, 2022–2026)

### Takeaway
I found no UWA paper that applies OLLA, an ACK-driven offset or adaptive conformal inference to control PER. In terrestrial cellular systems OLLA is the long-standing standard: an additive SINR offset driven by HARQ ACK/NACK toward a BLER target. Recent refinements exist (SALAD 2025). Conformal and calibration methods have been brought to wireless AI (Cohen–Park–Simeone–Shamai 2023; Simeone 2026). ACI with delayed feedback has a 2026 coverage theory (El Halabi–Brandt), but it is not applied to wireless. I found no paper that applies ACI or conformal risk control to link-adaptation PER under action-dependent feedback delay.

### Cited Findings
- OLLA: "OLLA maintains an additive offset variable that corrects the latest SINR estimate to drive the long-term BLER close to the target value"; it acts as an integrator on HARQ feedback. — [Wiesmayr et al., SALAD, arXiv 2510.05784](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05784); OLLA patents e.g. [US9379842](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/9379842)
- SALAD (Wiesmayr, Maggi, Cammerer, Hoydis, Aït Aoudia, Keller; arXiv 2510.05784, Oct 2025, rev. Mar 2026): "exclusively leverages ACK/NACK feedback to reliably track the evolution of the SINR… keeping the long-term BLER near a desired target". It infers SINR "by minimizing the cross-entropy loss between received ACK/NACKs and predicted BLER values" and has "a feedback control loop that adjusts the instantaneous BLER target". It "consistently outperforms… OLLA". The abstract does not address feedback delay. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05784)
- El Halabi & Brandt, "Adaptive Conformal Inference Under Delayed Feedback: Coverage Guarantees and a Delay-to-Memory Diagnostic," arXiv 2609.07251, Sept 2026. "The τ-delayed ACI recursion can be decomposed into τ interleaved ACI-like sequences, yielding a finite-sample bound on long-run empirical coverage with explicit dependence on τ." Not a wireless paper. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07251)
- Cohen, Park, Simeone, Shamai, "Calibrating AI Models for Wireless Communications via Conformal Prediction," IEEE TMLCN vol. 1, pp. 296–312, 2023. Simeone, "Conformal Calibration: Ensuring the Reliability of Black-Box AI in Wireless Systems," IEEE Communications Magazine, Jan 2026. These cover pre-deployment calibration and online monitoring. Whether either treats MCS/PER control was not verified. — [ResearchGate (TMLCN)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/374191597_Calibrating_AI_Models_for_Wireless_Communications_via_Conformal_Prediction); [ResearchGate (ComMag)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/390772345_Conformal_Calibration_Ensuring_the_Reliability_of_Black-Box_AI_in_Wireless_Systems)
- Related decision-calibration methods already curated locally (`curated/06_certified_link_adaptation/`): Angelopoulos et al., "Conformal PID Control for Time Series Prediction," NeurIPS 2023, DOI 10.52202/075280-1000; Lekeufack et al., "Conformal Decision Theory: Safe Autonomous Decisions from Imperfect Predictions," ICRA 2024, DOI 10.1109/ICRA57147.2024.10610041. Conformal decision theory calibrates a decision parameter online from realized losses, which is structurally the m_t update. — local DB
- TRACE-CRC, "Trajectory-Adaptive Conformal Risk Control for Multi-Step CSI Prediction," arXiv 2608.27124 (2026). Appeared in search; conformal risk control for multi-step CSI prediction. Not opened; whether it closes the loop to MCS selection is unknown. — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.27124)
- In UWA, a web search for OLLA or outer-loop link adaptation returned only terrestrial cellular sources and patents. The local library's "outer loop" query returns only vehicle-control papers, and "conformal" only non-comm hits plus the curated methods papers. — [search result set](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9860717/); local DB

### Inferences
- The margin update m_{k+1} = m_k + η(e_k − α) is the OLLA integrator, equivalently ACI on the miscoverage level, so the update rule itself is not novel (as the deck already states). What remains unclaimed by prior art found:
  - its use with an *action-dependent* ACK delay (≥ h + 2τ + T_a, which depends on N_sym through T_a), which El Halabi–Brandt do not cover (their delay is fixed);
  - its use in UWA;
  - its coupling to a physics-based predicted PER instead of an SINR offset.
- Reviewers will likely expect a citation to OLLA (3GPP practice, e.g., SALAD's background), ACI (Gibbs–Candès 2021), conformal decision theory (Lekeufack 2024) and delayed ACI (El Halabi–Brandt 2026).

### Gaps
- I did not find a terrestrial paper that combines (predictive SINR distribution → PER per action) with (OLLA/ACI margin) under explicit delayed feedback. It may exist in 5G URLLC or NTN literature not retrieved here. A targeted IEEE Xplore search ("OLLA" + "HARQ delay" / "feedback delay" + "NTN") is recommended.
- The content of the Cohen et al. 2023 and Simeone 2026 papers regarding link adaptation was not verified.

## Q4. Terrestrial Doppler-aware or mobility-aware link adaptation (V2X, HSR, LEO) as analogues; closeness ratings

### Takeaway
Terrestrial analogues use Doppler or velocity in three ways: to set measurement or update intervals (Samsung patent), as a feature for ML or DRL MCS and resource choices (V2X), or as a predicted quantity for compensation (HSR, LEO), rarely for the decision itself. Reliability-constrained MCS selection from a *predicted distribution* of SINR/interference exists in URLLC (Brighente et al. 2020; Belogaev et al. 2019 worst-case degradation at transmission time). None found uses a kinematic Doppler forecast to choose packet length and pilots with an ACK-calibrated PER margin. All are tangential or partial structural analogues.

### Cited Findings
- **Samsung / Seoul National Univ., US 8,654,822 B2, "Link adaptation system using doppler frequency and method using the same"** (priority Aug 2007, granted Feb 2014). Estimated Doppler sets the SNR measurement interval; claim 1: the unit "determine[s] the SNR measurement interval as a length of a transmission data packet when the estimated Doppler frequency is greater than or equal to the predetermined level." Closeness: tangential (Doppler sets the measurement cadence, no forecast or PER target). — [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8654822B2/en)
- **Belogaev, Khorov, Krasilov, Shmelkin, Tang, "Conservative Link Adaptation for URLLC," BlackSeaCom 2019, arXiv 1908.02227.** The algorithm "estimates the strongest channel degradation at the time moment of the actual packet transmission and selects an MCS taking into account the worst degradation". Closeness: partial structural analogue (predict degradation over the feedback delay, select MCS conservatively); no Doppler forecast, no packet length. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02227)
- **Brighente, Mohammadi, Baracca, "Interference Distribution Prediction for Link Adaptation in URLLC," IEEE VTC-Spring 2020, arXiv 2007.00306.** MCS choice "based on the statistics of the SINR… targeting the maximization of the SE while ensuring a certain target BLER". Closeness: partial structural analogue (predicted distribution → MCS under a BLER target), interference rather than Doppler. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.00306); [search snippet](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.00306)
- **CSI-prediction-for-LA papers** (arXiv 2511.20160, "CSI Prediction Frameworks for Enhanced 5G Link Adaptation"; arXiv 2510.24400). They frame "channel aging… user mobility and processing latency" as the cause of wrong MCS, with OLLA as the reactive baseline; one predicts in the effective-SINR (EESM) domain. Closeness: tangential (predict the channel, not the kinematics; MCS only). — [arXiv 2511.20160](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20160); [arXiv 2510.24400](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.24400)
- **V2X:** "Doppler-aware dynamic resource allocation for V2X communications using Double Deep Q-Network" (Elsevier 2025, S187449072500326X) and "PHY-Aware MCS Adaptation for NR V2X Mode 2 with ML-Driven Resource Allocation" (Research Square preprint, rs-7192344). The latter states that as Doppler grows, "SNR-only models increasingly overestimate channel reliability by lacking awareness of the growing inter-carrier interference component, selecting MCS indices that result in elevated packet error rate". Closeness: tangential to partial (Doppler-ICI-aware PER mapping, the same physical motivation as SNR_eff = SNR/(1+SNR·φ)). Not read in full. — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S187449072500326X); [Research Square PDF](https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-7192344/v1_covered_2ac9e13d-cd4d-418d-9642-047fe8cc7913.pdf)
- **HSR:** location-aware 5G with Doppler compensation, where "The velocity of trains can be predicted to high accuracy along the track", used for compensation, not link adaptation. Closeness: tangential (forecast-aided compensation, an analogue of Block 1, not Block 4). — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/318475419_Location-aware_5G_communications_and_Doppler_compensation_for_high-speed_train_networks)
- **MoFA** (Wi-Fi, mobility-aware A-MPDU length): see Q2. Closeness: partial analogue for the N_sym dimension. — [Google Patents US10477433](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10477433)

- Overall: on the evidence gathered, Block 4 has **not** been implemented in whole or in large part. Its parts (prediction ahead of feedback delay, PER/BER-target selection, uncertainty margin, ACK-driven offset, Doppler as a feature) each have precedents, so the paper should present Block 4 as a safety layer built from known parts (as the deck already does), with the new elements being the forecast-to-PER mapping over the receive interval, frame-layout actions, and the margin under action-dependent delay.

### Gaps
- LEO/NTN link adaptation using predicted Doppler (ephemeris-based) was not specifically searched. It is a plausible analogue in which Doppler is deterministic and predicted, and should be checked (e.g., "NTN link adaptation Doppler ephemeris HARQ disabled").
- The V2X DDQN and NR-V2X preprint were seen only via search snippets; the exact role of Doppler and any PER target were not verified.
- No browser or Google Scholar session was used, so patent families beyond the two found and IEEE Xplore-only papers may have been missed.
